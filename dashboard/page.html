Can the high and low be traded? Not on this evidence. Buying at the estimated low or selling at the estimated high and exiting at the close lost money after costs, even though the levels were reached as often as promised: being reached does not mean the price turns. What they are good for is sizing stops and judging how far a move can run.

Opening price from NSE's pre-open auction

Between 09:00 and 09:08 NSE collects orders and sets the opening price in a call auction; from 09:08 it is final and becomes the official open at 09:15. Reading it is not a forecast, it is the opening price itself, available about seven minutes early. It says nothing by itself about whether the day goes up or down. Run python3 src/preopen.py at 09:09, or ask me. The order-book imbalance in the table is only logged; whether it means anything is untested (on 15 Oct sellers outnumbered buyers 3 to 1 and the stock still rose 2.7%).

What would change the forecast

  • A SEBI circular on derivative settlement and the closing-auction price. Last-30-minute VWAP favours the bullish scenario; keeping or blending the auction price favours the bearish one. Its timing is unknown and the model does not score it.
  • A pre-open price outside the model's 5–95% opening band. Then condition on the open.
  • A large overnight move in US or Asian markets. The validated link to the gap is real but small and unstable.
  • Any tax, fee, margin or expiry change for exchanges, or a holiday or trading halt notice.
  • A directional rule that passes a chronological test in the closing-auction era, and still pays after costs, would upgrade the verdict.

Known limits

  • No TradingView chart, indicator or screenshot was inspected. The supplied workbook is Yahoo Finance API data.
  • v2 was written after the session opened. Its information set is limited by rule, not by the clock.
  • No order book, flow, short-interest or GIFT Nifty data. Few sessions exist since the closing auction began.
  • The cause of the 13 Oct afternoon volume spike and of the 14 Oct decline was not established.
  • No separate red-team pass was run on the integrated report.
