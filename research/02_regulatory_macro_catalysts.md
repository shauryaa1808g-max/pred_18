## (a) Executive summary

1. **Price context.** BSE Ltd closed Rs 3,375.40 on 13 Oct (+1.36%; Rs 3,330.20 on 12 Oct, +1.31%). 52-week high Rs 4,446.80 (intraday 27 May 2026; best close Rs 4,403.30 on 26 May), 52-week low Rs 2,326.10. The stock is ~23% below the peak close, +10.8% above the 1 Oct closing low (Rs 3,046.00), and has had daily close-to-close sigma of ~2.1% (65 sessions since 13 Jul; 12 sessions with |move| >3%, 27 with >2%; last 20 sessions sigma 2.35%) [C]. Beta to Nifty since April ~0.96 but correlation only ~0.37 [C]: moves are mostly stock/sector-specific (SEBI/RBI/NSE headlines), not index beta.
2. **The live regulatory catalyst is SEBI's Closing Auction Session (CAS) rollback.** CAS began 3 Aug 2026 for ~220 F&O stocks and made expiry-day derivative settlement depend on a thin auction; index-option volumes fell (NSE Aug index-option premium turnover -28.6% m/m; Reuters/Jefferies ~-20% m/m options ADTO; BSE flagged weak CAS liquidity). SEBI published a consultation paper on 12 Sep (comments closed 3 Oct, ~20,000 comments). Reuters (dated 5 Oct, unnamed sources) says SEBI is likely to stop using CAS for derivative settlement for at least a year and revert to the final-30-minute VWAP, effective by end-October. SEBI Chairman Pandey said on **Sat 10 Oct** "We are currently examining the comments and hope to issue guidelines in about a week's time" (so ~17 Oct; could drop on 14 Oct). As of SEBI's circular listing fetched on 13 Oct the newest circular is dated 7 Oct and there is no CAS circular. The market has partly priced a favourable outcome (BSE +4.39% / +3.94% / +1.54% on 5/6/7 Oct; +1.31% and +1.36% on 12/13 Oct; note BSE +1.36% on 13 Oct lagged the Nifty's +1.60%). Outcome space is not symmetric: pure 30-min VWAP = what the Reuters leak says; blended VWAP (SEBI "Option 1") or CAS retained = less favourable than the leak.
3. **NSE is now listed (24 Sep 2026, listed at Rs 1,800 on BSE vs issue price Rs 1,785)** and trades below issue (Rs 1,722.45 at 11:54 on 13 Oct; market cap ~Rs 4.28 lakh crore vs BSE ~Rs 1.38 lakh crore). It gives investors a pure-play comparable (BSE P/E TTM 48.5x vs NSE ~45.7x) and disclosed share data: NSE share of equity-options premium turnover fell to 68.48% in Q1 FY27 from 78.63% a year earlier (so BSE ~31.5%).
4. **BSE entered the Nifty 50 (replacing Wipro) after the close of 29 Sep 2026.** Passive buying (~US$0.64-0.74 bn estimates) is already done; the stock did "sell the news": +3.31% on 29 Sep on 30.1m shares (vs ~3-7m normal), then -3.37% (30 Sep), -1.49% (1 Oct).

Pandey's last explicit comments (31 Oct 2025) were "cannot just shut down weekly F&O expiries"; on 22 Sep and 10 Oct 2026 he did not mention weekly expiry. Tail risk remains a headline risk (history: -7.6% on 21 Aug 2025, -4.6% on 11 Sep 2025 on reports/comments alone).
