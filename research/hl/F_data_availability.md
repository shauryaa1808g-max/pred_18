Corrections there override wording above (volumes are bonus-adjusted; 19 results dates; NSE's own circular fixes the pre-open timetable: the open is fixed at a random moment 09:08 to 09:10).**

*Status: COMPLETE (written Sat 17 Oct 2026, probes run 08:06 to 08:45 UTC, i.e. 13:36 to 14:15 IST). Scope: AVAILABILITY and HISTORY LENGTH only. No predictive claim is made anywhere in this file; where a ranking appears it ranks how ready the data is, not how useful it will be.*

## 0. Rules this study followed (fixed before the first probe)

* Public GET requests only, through the sandbox proxy. No credentials, no logins, nothing behind a login. A handful of requests per source for probing; NSE was reached with the one-visit cookie warm-up of `src/preopen.py` and never looped.
* "Reachable" means the proxy let the request through AND the answer was real data (HTTP 200 with a recognisable body). 403/404/empty is reported as such.
* Nothing dated after 15 Oct 2026 22:00 IST was written into any results file (the builders in `src/hl_fetch_extra.py` refuse `--to` after 2026-10-15 and drop later announcement rows). Live endpoints can only show "the last session" (probe clock: Saturday 17 Oct, so they showed Friday 16 Oct); I used them to learn the format only and stored none of their values as study data. Raw probe downloads sit in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.
* Every file under `results/hl4_*` carries provenance: URL, retrieval time (UTC), sha256 (the `*_meta.json` next to each CSV).
* Effort scale: S = under an hour, M = about a day, L = several days, X = needs money or an agreement.

## 2. Item by item

### 2.1 NSE pre-open market history

**What exists.** `https://www.nseindia.com/api/market-data-pre-open?key=ALL` (needs the cookie warm-up) returns all ~2,100 pre-open rows for the **latest** pre-open only. For BSE the row has: `IEP`, `finalPrice`, `finalQuantity`, `totalBuyQuantity`, `totalSellQuantity`, `atoBuyQty`, `atoSellQty`, `prevClose`, `lastUpdateTime` and the price ladder (`preopen`, 10 price levels). I tested a date parameter (`&date=14-Oct-2026`): the answer was byte-identical to the latest snapshot (timestamp 16-Oct-2026 09:08:59), so the endpoint has **no history**. The snapshot stays on the endpoint until the next pre-open starts.

**Archive search.** NSE's own catalogue of daily reports (`https://www.nseindia.com/api/merged-daily-reports?key=favCapital`, 9 capital-market files such as UDiFF bhavcopy, Market Activity Report, delivery positions, price-band changes, daily volatility) contains **no pre-open file**. I also opened the Market Activity Report (`archives/equities/mkt/MA151026.csv`, 235 KB) and the PR bhavcopy zip (`archives/equities/bhavcopy/pr/PR151026.zip`, 14 files): neither contains a pre-open field. The quote endpoint `api/quote-equity?symbol=BSE` (which can carry `preOpenMarket`) answered 403 from the sandbox. Web searches found no free archive; the one public forum thread I read says nobody collects it. NSE Data and Analytics sells real-time and historical feeds (price list not found; unverified); a retail data vendor advertises IEP and volume for 09:00 to 09:08 (vendor claim, untested, paid).

**What the project already has for free:** the official open (= the final IEP, from the bhavcopy) and the previous close. It does **not** have the order totals, the indicative volume or the path of the IEP.

**Flag for the lead (timing rule).** Three secondary sources (a news site, a broker help page, a broker news page; I did not retrieve the NSE circular) say the NSE pre-open structure changed from **7 Sep 2026**: orders (market and limit) 09:00 to 09:05, limit orders only 09:05 to 09:10 with a random close in the last two minutes, matching 09:10 to 09:12, buffer to 09:15, aligned with the closing auction that began 3/4 Aug 2026. One broker page describes it differently (entry to 09:08 then a random close up to 09:10), so the details disagree. The project's tools assume "final at 09:08" (`src/preopen.py`, `docs/LIVE_TOOLS.md`). The one saved snapshot in the repo (15 Oct) has `lastUpdateTime` 09:09:34 and finalPrice equal to the official open, and the live endpoint's last stamp I saw was 09:08:59 (16 Oct), so the open was final by about 09:09:30 on those days, but **do not read the open as final before about 09:10:30 until the NSE circular is checked.** This does not change any backtest (the official open comes from the bhavcopy); it changes when the post-open numbers can be shown live.

**How to get history from tomorrow.** `python3 src/hl_fetch_extra.py snap-preopen` at 09:01, 09:04, 09:07, 09:09, 09:11 IST (five calls, BSE + 8 peers + market totals, about 2 KB each compressed). Tested once (it returned the 16 Oct snapshot because it was a Saturday). At about 250 sessions a year, n=100 is reached about 5 months after the first logged session (mid-March 2027) and n=250 about October 2027. Until then any pre-open-book feature is untestable.

**Fetched.** `results/hl4_upstox_15m_BSE.csv`: **29,555 rows, 1,187 sessions, 2022-01-03 to 2026-10-15**, 58 monthly requests (all HTTP 200, 3 s apart), sha256 `a7e5010d399015886bcdea2413c71c847a01353ac4faeb2ed874716c985adc0f`. 1,181 of the sessions are the official NSE sessions in the project's daily file (every official session is present, none missing); 6 are special sessions that the daily file does not have (4 evening Muhurat sessions with 4 bars: 2022-10-24, 2023-11-12, 2024-11-01, 2025-10-21; 2 Saturday special sessions with 7 bars: 2024-03-02, 2024-05-18). All 1,181 regular sessions have exactly 25 fifteen-minute bars.

**Quality check (`results/hl4_upstox_check.csv`, `..._check_summary.json`).** The bars are **price-adjusted for later bonus issues**: the official close divided by the Upstox close snaps to a factor of **9 until 17 Mar 2022 (52 sessions), 3 from 21 Mar 2022 to 22 May 2025 (782 sessions), 1 from 23 May 2025 (347 sessions)**. The two change dates are exactly the ex-dates of the two 2:1 bonus issues in the project's own `data/corp_actions_verified.csv` (21 Mar 2022 and 23 May 2025, factor 3 each). After multiplying by the factor and comparing with the official NSE daily bar (raw traded prices):

| Era (n regular sessions) | Daily high matches official within 5 paise | Daily low | Median absolute error of high / low | Largest error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Factor 1, 23 May 2025 to 15 Oct 2026 (347) | **100%** | **100%** | Rs 0.00 / Rs 0.00 | Rs 0.00 |
| Factor 3, 21 Mar 2022 to 22 May 2025 (782) | 20% | 25% | Rs 0.45 (0.020%) / Rs 0.45 (0.021%) | high Rs 7.20 (1.02%, 14 Jun 2022), low Rs 12.50 (1.88%, 13 Jun 2022); 91% of sessions within 0.1% on both; 99% within 0.25% |
| Factor 9, 3 Jan to 17 Mar 2022 (52) | 12% | 8% | Rs 1.03 / Rs 1.50 | high Rs 4.00, low Rs 8.50 (0.42%) |

So for the post-bonus era the intraday file reproduces the official high and low exactly; before that the adjusted prices are rounded to coarse ticks (error shrinks from 0.055% median in 2022 to 0.009% in 2025), which is negligible against a typical daily range of 3 to 4%, but the two June 2022 days are 1 to 2% off and should be dropped or checked. Open matches the official open on 100% of the 347 factor-1 sessions. The official close matches the last 15-minute bar on only 16% of those sessions because, before the closing auction began (3 Aug 2026), NSE's official close was, to my knowledge, a volume-weighted average of the last 30 minutes of trades rather than the last trade (not re-verified here; the 16% against 100% is the measured fact); **since 3 Aug 2026 (n=52) the last bar's close, high and low match the official bar on 100%**. Volumes in the file are share counts and are not adjusted for the bonus (ratio to official volume reflects raw shares; not used here).

**What this changes.** The repo's 15-minute history was 37 sessions (`data/processed/intraday_union_15m.csv`, 18 Aug to 13 Oct 2026) plus hourly bars. The live high/low tool and the hour-by-hour table were calibrated on those sessions (the project's own notes say about 39). A 1,181-session 15-minute history makes those calibrations testable. That is a statement about sample size, not about accuracy.

### 2.4 GIFT Nifty pre-open levels

* **Live:** `https://www.nseindia.com/api/marketStatus` has a `giftnifty` object (fields `LASTPRICE`, `TIMESTMP`, `DAYCHANGE`, `PERCHANGE`, `EXPIRYDATE`, ...) and an `indicativenifty50` object (the indicative Nifty during the pre-open). **Both were null on Saturday 17 Oct**, so I could not confirm they populate on a trading morning. The logger records them (`snap-market` at 08:30 and 08:55 IST, and with every `snap-preopen`). Two guessed NSE paths (`api/giftNifty`, `api/gift-nifty`) returned 404.
* **NSE International Exchange (the exchange that trades it):** `https://www.nseix.com/` is a JavaScript application that calls its own JSON API with an anonymous page token (`/api/generate-token`, no login; the page does the same for every visitor). Its bundle lists `/api/historical-derivative?fromdate=DD-Mon-YYYY&todate=...&symbol=...&instrumenttype=...` (daily contract-wise OHLC, settlement price, open interest) and `/api/getquotes/intraday`. I made two history queries for Nifty futures (1 to 15 Oct 2026, instrument types `FUTIDX` and `FUT`): HTTP 200 with the column list but **zero data rows**, so I could not confirm the parameter names and stopped (the token handshake is the site's own anonymous flow; I did not go further). Even if it works it is daily data, not the 08:45 level.
* **Third parties:** a market-data blog notes the contract was SGX Nifty before July 2023 and GIFT Nifty after, with different session times, so any long series is two series spliced. No free downloadable intraday history was found. Vendors (TradingView, investing sites, paid feeds) were not tested.
* **Verdict:** no verified history. For the pre-open forecast, the project already has the US close and USDINR; GIFT Nifty is the missing Asian-hours piece. Log it from tomorrow; do not plan on a back-test.

### 2.7 FII/DII daily flows

* **Cash-market FII/DII net (NSE):** `https://www.nseindia.com/api/fiidiiTradeReact` returns the latest session only (two rows: DII and FII/FPI with buy, sell, net; I passed `date=14-Oct-2026` and got the same 16 Oct rows back, so no history). NSDL's FPI "Latest" page (`https://www.fpi.nsdl.co.in/web/Reports/Latest.aspx`, 59 KB) also shows only the latest day (equity, debt, hybrid, MF, derivative trades); the archive page is a form (postback), the other NSDL URL I tried was rejected, and SEBI's FPI page returned its homepage. **No bulk dated history of cash flows was obtained.** Third-party sites list them (unverified).

## 4. Which of these could realistically improve the high/low, and what to log

No predictive value is claimed; this is a ranking of readiness and of the mechanism's plausibility, to be tested only with the project's pre-fixed rule.

1. **Intraday history (2.2, 2.3): the only item that changes what can be tested now.** The project's live tools (live high/low bands, hour-by-hour table, next-hour model) were calibrated on 37 to 39 sessions; B found nothing helped there, which with n=37 means "cannot tell", not "does not help". With 1,181 sessions of 15-minute BSE, Nifty and India VIX bars, those studies can be re-run with a power that means something, and the time of day of the high and low and the first-15-minute range become measurable. It does not add new information to the 09:08 forecast itself.
2. **Results-date flag (2.6): known weeks ahead and exact, but only 18 reaction days since 2022 (5 in the Final window).** Usable as a caution flag, not as a tested feature. Next: Thu 5 Nov 2026.
3. **Intraday option-chain snapshots (2.5): plausible mechanism (implied vol is the market's own range forecast) but 464 end-of-day sessions is all the history that can exist; intraday IV starts accumulating tomorrow.** The existing daily IV was already tested in D on its own window.
4. **Pre-open book (2.1): mechanism plausible only for the post-open numbers (the IEP already summarises the book); needs about 100 sessions (mid-March 2027) before any test.**
5. **GIFT Nifty (2.4): the missing overnight piece for the pre-open forecast, but no verified source of values yet.**
6. **Participant positions and cash FII/DII (2.7): cheap to get; no obvious link to the size of the day's range for one stock; lowest priority.** Participant history is the only item here that could be back-filled and tested at once.
7. **Index-rebalance dates (2.6): the 29 Sep 2026 close (high = close, volume 30.1m shares per the project's notes) is the one clear event; two dated events (this one and the Nov 2024 MSCI addition, the latter from a secondary source) cannot be tested.** Next dated ones: MSCI effective 1 Dec 2026 (trade at the 30 Nov close).
