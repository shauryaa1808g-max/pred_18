### Defects

* Volume is adjusted but documented as unadjusted (see corrections); a builder following the report will compare bar volume with raw official volume and be off by 3x / 9x, and by 6x / 18x on 11 sessions where bar volume is doubled.
* Structural break at the closing auction (3 Aug 2026): the 15:15 bar's share of day volume drops from a median 7.3% (2025-05-23..2026-07-31) to 0.7% (3 Aug..15 Oct 2026; 0.3-11% before the break, 0.3-6.1% after except 29 Sep 2026, 65%), and the 15:00 bar rises from 5.0% to 7.2%. The 15:15 bar now carries the auction print (its close = official close 52/52). F reports the close match but not this change in what the last bar is; 'last 15-minute bar' features are not comparable across 3 Aug 2026.
* Adjusted-era prices sit on a coarse 0.5 grid (every one of the 29,555-8,679 pre-23-May-2025 bars is a multiple of 0.5 on the adjusted scale = Rs 1.5 raw at factor 3, Rs 4.5 at factor 9). Consequences F did not state: 4.2% (2022) and 5.2% (2023) of bars are flat (high==low) vs 0.1% from 2024; the day's high is touched by more than one bar on 19% (2022) and 22% (2023) of sessions and the day's low on 44% (2022) and 34% (2023) vs 3.6% in 2026, so time-of-high/low is ambiguous before 2024.
* One internally inconsistent bar: 2024-06-25 09:15 has open 838.0 < low 839.0 (independent rounding of O/H/L); the day's own high/low are unaffected.
* check_upstox() derives the factor from the official close/bar close ratio, not from the corporate-actions file (it happens to agree on all 1,181 sessions, so no numerical effect). BSE meta file name (hl4_upstox_15m_meta.json) differs from the CSV name and lacks the 'symbol' key, because it was produced by the first revision of upstox(); HEAD code would not regenerate an identical meta.
* Terms: the availability table 'terms' field says 'no redistribution; tolerated not guaranteed' without a source; Upstox terms of use were not read by F (nor by me).

### Overclaims

* '91% ... within 0.1% on both' reads as a joint statement; jointly it is 85.3%.
* 'Volumes ... not adjusted for the bonus' is stated as fact and is false.
* '4 evening Muhurat sessions' is wrong for 2025-10-21.
* 'Bar extremes before 2024 are sampled values that understate the official range slightly': the Nifty pattern (bar high below, bar low above on most 2022 sessions) is confirmed, but the cause (sampling) is F's inference, not shown. For BSE equity the bar-minus-official range bias is about zero (median -0.003 pp), so the understatement is an index-file property, not a BSE one.
* 'Official close ... was, to my knowledge, a volume-weighted average of the last 30 minutes' is flagged by F as not re-verified; my approximate test (bar-derived VWAP) is consistent with it but cannot confirm the exact rule.
* 'Done / the one clear win' for the intraday history is about availability; the file is a vendor snapshot adjusted to the 15 Oct 2026 share basis and any later corporate action will rescale all history on a re-fetch, which the report does not mention.

### Operational flags for the live pipeline

* Do not use bar volume against bse_daily vol without dividing by the factor (3 or 9); 11 sessions have bar volume about twice that (2022-03-02, 2022-06-30, 2022-08-29, 2022-10-28, 2022-12-27, 2023-04-26, 2023-08-24, 2023-10-23, 2023-12-22, 2024-02-20, 2024-06-19).
* Bars as served are on the adjusted share basis; official OHLC levels are raw and official 'prev' is adjusted only one step. Any join with bse_daily levels must multiply bar prices by 9 / 3 / 1 by date (ex-dates 2022-03-21, 2025-05-23). Relative features inside one era need no factor.
* Keep the stored files frozen (hashes above). A future bonus or split would make Upstox rescale ALL history on the next fetch, so appending new months to this file without re-deriving the factor table would corrupt it. The files stop at 15 Oct by design; the 16 Oct session is not in them (and I did not test that the endpoint serves it).
* The Upstox endpoint works without a token only by tolerance (its docs require a bearer token; docs page carries an API/algo regulation banner). A live Monday pipeline should not depend on it without a fallback.
* Closing auction break at 3 Aug 2026: exclude or separately model the 15:15 bar and last-bar timing features; pre-auction, removing the 15:15 bar changes the day high on 32/295 and the day low on 38/295 sessions.
* 6 special dates (2022-10-24, 2023-11-12, 2024-03-02, 2024-05-18, 2024-11-01, 2025-10-21) are in the bars and in index_daily.csv but not in bse_daily*.csv: filter on the official session list before joining. Sat 2024-01-20, Sat 2025-02-01, Sun 2026-02-01 are full 25-bar official sessions on weekend dates (day-of-week dummies, calendar gaps).
* Bars are complete only 15 minutes after their stamp: a feature built from the HH:MM bar is not knowable before HH:MM+15. The 09:15 bar's open is the official opening price (pre-open auction result).
* Pre-2024 adjusted bars: 4-5% flat bars and frequent ties for the day's high/low bar; avoid time-of-extreme or bar-range estimators before 2024 or check robustness to them. Nifty bars before 2024 should not be used as official daily extremes; use data/processed/index_daily.csv.
* index_daily.csv ends 12 Oct 2026, so Nifty/VIX bars for 13-15 Oct cannot be validated there (15 Oct validated by hand against the NSE index file, exact for Nifty).
* Scratch note: the shared scratchpad folder verF already contained exp1.py, nse.sh, nse_jar.txt that are not mine; I created only files named common.py, s1..s5 scripts, *.pkl and raw/ there. No tracked repo file was modified and nothing was committed.

### Defects

* hl_fetch_extra.py has no loop: every snap-preopen call is a single shot, so scheduler delay of up to 6 minutes moves single-shot cron lines out of the 09:08-09:11 window.
* snap_preopen on 403/empty/invalid JSON logs nothing and returns exit 0 (verified with mocked curl): gaps are invisible; snap_market/snap_chain log http 0 (real status lost).
* Worst-case runtime of a failing call about 232 s (nse_warm up to 25 s x4, curl up to 40 s x3, sleeps 12 s); nse_json also sleeps after the final failed try.
* Cookie warm-up (homepage hit) is repeated on every call; once per job is enough (one run of snap-market took 28.8 s, cause undetermined).
* Loggers are not idempotent (append): duplicate lines on a double start; the stale/holiday snapshot is written under today's date and detected only afterwards by `status` (and `status` also flags event_calendar_BSE as STALE because its newest date is a future meeting, 04-Nov-2026: cosmetic false positive).
* fetched_utc is written after the fetch and the request start time is not stored; the lag between wall clock and the exchange stamp (48 s and 56 s on 16 Oct) cannot be measured from the logs without it.
* results/hl4_log/ is not in .gitignore (only data/raw/, __pycache__, *.pyc, .venv/ are); F's text says to keep it out of git.
* Paths are relative (results/hl4_log, data/processed/...): the scheduler must run from /home/user/pred_18; JAR is a fixed /tmp path shared by concurrent runs.
* src/preopen.py: no stale-date check, accepts finalPrice>0 with finalQuantity 0, no 'no price discovered' state, saves Friday's record again when run on a weekend or before the new session starts.
* src/build_dashboard_data.py:162 still decides 'final' by the clock only (stamp >= 09:08:00) and takes IEP; with only an early snapshot saved it would show an indicative price as the pre-open price.
* Stale 09:08 wording remains in docs/LIVE_TOOLS.md (lines 18, 20, 34), src/hourly_table.py (298, 300), comments in hl_model.py, hl_analog.py, hl_ml.py, hl_model2.py, docs/INPUT_AUDIT.md:17, docs/V2_BRIEF.md:5, src/finalize_record.py:17.
