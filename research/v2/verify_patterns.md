## 5. Findings that qualify the report (all minor; none changes a forecast input)
1. **VWAP-close explanation of the lag-1 autocorrelation is not supported by repo data.** `bse_daily.csv` has the last traded price (`last`, differs from `close` on 96% of sessions). Lag-1 ACF of last-to-last log returns is +0.092 versus +0.105 for close-to-close (EARLY 0.108 vs 0.141; L1 0.063 vs 0.036; auction era 0.018 for both). A back-of-envelope MA(1) calculation for a 30-minute average inside a 375-minute day gives about w/6/375 = 0.013. So a VWAP close can account for roughly 0.013 of the 0.105, not for most of it. The open-to-open zero is equally compatible with transient opening-auction noise (gaps fade: slope -0.16 EARLY) depressing the open-to-open autocorrelation. The report calls the VWAP story an untested hypothesis, but the wording "consistent with a close-definition component" and the "dismiss as artefact if auction-era ACF stays near zero" criterion lean on it, and a discriminating test was available in the data. Verdict on that sub-claim: not supported.
2. **"Data-supported hedge: widen a 50% interval by 10%"** rests on an unregistered pooled statistic (kappa50 1.098 [1.024, 1.157], Kupiec p 0.005 would not survive any registry-wide adjustment) and a stressed-class cell (family Holm 0.041, whole registry 0.34). The numbers reproduce and the cluster bootstrap supports a real shortfall, so "plausible, optional" is fair; "data-supported" is a notch strong. The 90% interval needs no change (stressed 0.872, Kupiec p 0.076, kappa90 1.054 [0.957, 1.263]).
3. **4b "price impact" is mostly mechanical.** A = |R|/turnover contains turnover in the denominator: if |R| were independent of turnover the Spearman(ln A, TZ) would be about -0.28 (my permutation null); the observed -0.129 is weaker, i.e. |R| rises with turnover. The sentence "Amihud impact per rupee was lower on heavy-turnover days before 2024-07" is therefore a restatement of the volume-|return| link, not independent evidence on liquidity.
4. **Status label compression.** `not_found` is used for P-03 (yesterday's return, pooled whole-registry Holm 0.052, RC_PD 0.031, OOS positive in 7 of 8 years) and P-13 (turnover -> |R|/sigma, whole-registry Holm 0.0025) because they fail the Final gate. The body text explains this correctly; the one-word register entry understates that a nominal OOS effect exists.
5. **Cell autocorrelations are centred on the full-sample mean** (as pre-registered), which inflates a cell's ACF when the cell mean differs from the global mean. With within-cell centring my values are PV-lo 0.100 (vs 0.165), PD near-high 0.121 (vs 0.130), deep 0.154 (vs 0.162), PN 0.0965/0.1245. No conclusion changes (between-regime differences are not significant either way; the pooled "all" cell is unaffected), but the "PV-lo +0.165" figure is partly a mean-offset artefact.
6. **Provenance not independently checkable:** pre-registration timestamp (header precedes first run) can only be taken on trust; the earlier scratchpad attempt was opened after results were final (disclosed); agreement with it is not independent. The session was live when the analyst worked (07:15-07:55 UTC on 14 Oct); no 14-Oct row exists in any input file, so this is not leakage.
