## A. Executive summary

1. State of the business: Q1 FY27 (Apr-Jun 2026), announced 4 Aug 2026 after market, was a record quarter. Consolidated revenue from operations Rs 1,566 cr (+63.5% YoY, only +0.2% QoQ), total income Rs 1,707 cr, PAT Rs 872.7 cr (+62.2% YoY, +9.7% QoQ), EPS Rs 21.22. Transaction charges were 84.8% of operating revenue. Growth was flat sequentially on revenue because Q4 FY26 had already stepped up.
2. The stock is trading on regulation and volume trend, not on reported earnings. Since the print (4 Aug close Rs 3,618) the stock is -6.7%. Drivers: (a) SEBI's Closing Auction Session (CAS) went live 3 Aug 2026 and BSE index-options turnover fell sharply (Nuvama: Aug average daily premium traded Rs 18,100 cr, lowest since Jan 2025, vs Rs 29,615 cr in Q1); (b) STT hike (Budget, 1 Feb 2026, in force 1 Apr 2026); (c) RBI bank-guarantee and prop-funding curbs in force 1 Jul 2026; (d) analyst downgrades (Jefferies to Underperform 17 Aug, Nuvama to Hold 18 Aug, Bernstein initiated Underperform 9 Sep).
3. The most forecast-relevant live catalyst: SEBI's final CAS / expiry-settlement guidelines. SEBI chairman said on 10 Oct 2026 that guidelines should come "in about a week". Reuters (6 Oct) reported SEBI is likely to move derivatives settlement back to the last-30-minute VWAP for at least a year, effective by end-October. The stock rose +4.4% (5 Oct) and +3.9% (6 Oct) and is +10.8% from the 1 Oct close of Rs 3,046, so some relief is priced; the final text could still move it either way. Window for the circular: roughly 14-20 Oct (unconfirmed).
4. Q2 FY27 (Jul-Sep 2026) results date: NOT published as of 13 Oct. History: board-meeting intimations came 17-27 days before the meeting and Q2 results came on 12 Nov 2024 and 11 Nov 2025. Likely window for results is first half of Nov 2026 (stockanalysis estimate 6 Nov, algorithmic, UNVERIFIED). Trading window for insiders normally shuts at quarter-end (from 1 Oct).
