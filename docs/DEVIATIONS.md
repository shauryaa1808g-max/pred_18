model output on real BSE data had been produced** (only synthetic-data self-tests had been run).

| 14 | 2026-10-13 ~16:30 | addition (exploratory, descriptive only) | 15-minute bars for the last ~60 sessions are obtained from Yahoo's chart API through a summarising WebFetch (direct curl is rate-limited). | 15 | 2026-10-13 ~17:00 | **post-hoc extension (rule fixed before computing)** | After the descriptive conditional-day table showed a positive full-sample median gap (+0.28%) and negative mean intraday move, the "historical-average" location benchmark L1 (un-centred standardised residuals, i.e. location = trailing-500 median of z × σ) is evaluated for the G, I and R targets, not only R as in the first run. **Decision rule fixed now:** L1 replaces L0 for a target iff on Validation+Final pooled its mean pinball is lower with DM one-sided p < 0.05 AND L1 is better (point estimate) in Validation and in Final separately. Otherwise L0 stays. This is a post-hoc extension motivated by descriptive output, so its result is treated as weaker evidence than the pre-registered gates. | Yes (descriptive means of G, I seen) |
| 16 | 2026-10-13 ~17:15 | **judgement override (disclosed)** | Rule #15 selected L1 (positive gap median ≈ +0.3%) for the G target on Validation+Final. For the **live** forecast this is overridden and the gap median stays at 0 (L0), because a structural break was found: from 2026-08-03 the NSE close is the closing-auction price (bhavcopy `LAST == CLOSE` every day; before that CLOSE was a 30-minute VWAP and differed from LAST), and in the 49 sessions since then the share of positive gaps is 0.449 (vs 0.707 before; two-sided binomial p = 0.0002), the median gap is 0.000% and 16% of gaps are exactly zero. The historical gap premium most likely came from the VWAP-close convention and no longer applies. Caveat: n = 49 post-break sessions; the mean-gap difference is not significant by Welch t (p = 0.11), only the sign share and median shift are. | Yes |
| 17 | 2026-10-13 ~17:30 | data-validation rule (exploratory source) | With 38 intraday sessions fetched, closes matched the exchange close within 0.00 on every session, but first-bar opens differed from NSE's official open by up to ₹22 (0.7%) on gap days (NSE open = pre-open auction price; bar open = first continuous trade), highs fell short by ≤0.16% (missed ticks) and volume ran 83–99% of NSE's (no auction volume). The validator now tests authenticity and the quantities used: close within 0.02%, high/low within 0.25%, volume ratio 0.80–1.02, ≥24 bars. The open is reported, not tested, and bar opens are never used as the session open. Changed after seeing the failures. | Yes |
| 18 | 2026-10-13 ~17:45 | data-validation rule (exploratory source) | The collecting agent reported that the summarising WebFetch layer sometimes returned garbled rows (columns shifted by one row; low above close; zero volume) that aggregate checks would not always catch. Added row-level tests: per-bar OHLC consistency (±₹0.06), volume > 0 (except the final 15:15 bar, which legitimately shows 0 volume in the closing-auction window — first version of this test wrongly flagged it), and bar-open within 0.4% of the previous bar's close. Sessions failing any row test are excluded. Intraday statistics remain descriptive and non-gating. | Yes |
